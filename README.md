## 📌 Project Overview
**StudySmart** is a high-fidelity mobile application prototype designed to optimize student productivity while actively preventing academic burnout. By integrating the **Pomodoro technique** with behavioral wellness features, the app provides a balanced approach to deep work.

### **The Problem**
Students often struggle with digital distractions and "burnout" caused by long, unstructured study sessions without adequate breaks.

### **The Solution**
A User-Centered Design (UCD) approach that combines time-tracking analytics with suggested physical and mental "Quick Breaks" to keep the mind fresh and efficient.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### **1. Smart Study Timer**
* Customizable focus and break durations (defaulting to 25/5 minute intervals).
* Session progress tracking with the ability to pause, resume, or end sessions manually.

### **2. Healthy Break Ecosystem**
* **Quick Break Ideas:** Integrated suggestions such as "Mindful Minute," "Quick Stretch," and "Short Walk".
* **Hydration Reminders:** Automated prompts to ensure physical well-being during long study blocks.

### **3. Performance Analytics**
* **Weekly Overview:** Visual charts comparing total "Focus Time" vs. "Break Time".
* **Progress Tracking:** Daily goals and session history to monitor long-term efficiency trends.

### **4. Distraction Management**
* **App Blocking:** Ability to restrict access to Social Media, Messaging, and Entertainment apps during active focus sessions.
* **Break Notifications:** Controlled alerts to ensure users return to work without over-extending their rest periods.
